Privacy Policy for Audio Backup

This Privacy Policy explains how Audio Backup handles your permissions and data.

1. Device Permissions

The app requests audio read permission (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) only to detect audio files on your device.

2. Google Sign-In & Google Drive Access

The app uses Google Sign-In and the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope solely to upload those audio files to your own Google Drive.

3. No Third-Party Servers

No audio files, personal data, or tokens are sent to or stored on any third-party server.

4. Data Retention & Revoking Access

Original recordings remain on your device, and you can revoke access anytime from your Google Account permissions.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: vitthalpatidarrr@gmail.com
