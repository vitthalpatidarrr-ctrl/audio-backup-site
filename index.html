Audio Backup

Audio Backup is an Android app that automatically backs up audio recordings from your device to your own Google Drive.

Key Features

  • Automatically detects audio recordings on your Android device.
  • Securely uploads your recordings directly to your personal Google Drive.
  • Keeps your original recordings safe on your device.
  • No third-party servers — your files stay strictly between your device and your Google Drive.
